# Groww ELSS Tax Saver Fund Direct Growth

## Official Scheme Overview & Key Facts
- **Fund House / AMC:** Groww Mutual Fund
- **Category:** Equity / ELSS
- **Benchmark:** NIFTY 500 Total Return Index
- **Expense Ratio (Direct Growth):** 1.47% (Total Expense Ratio as declared by AMC)
- **Exit Load:** Nil
- **Statutory Lock-in Period:** 3 Years
- **Minimum SIP Investment:** ₹500
- **Minimum Lump-sum Investment:** ₹500
- **Stamp Duty:** 0.005% (from July 1st, 2020)
- **Fund Manager(s):** Anupam Tiwari
- **Launch Date:** 28-Dec-2017

## Investment Objective & Description
The Scheme seeks to generate long-term capital appreciation from a diversified portfolio of predominantly equity and equity-related Securities. The scheme shall offer tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.
