# Groww Liquid Fund Direct Growth

## Official Scheme Overview & Key Facts
- **Fund House / AMC:** Groww Mutual Fund
- **Category:** Debt / Liquid
- **Benchmark:** CRISIL Liquid Debt A-I Index
- **Expense Ratio (Direct Growth):** 0.11% (Total Expense Ratio as declared by AMC)
- **Exit Load:** Exit load of 0.0070% if redeemed within 1 day, 0.0065% if redeemed within 2 days, 0.0060% if redeemed within 3 days, 0.0055% if redeemed within 4 days, 0.0050% if redeemed within 5 days, 0.0045% if redeemed within 6 days.
- **Statutory Lock-in Period:** Nil (No lock-in)
- **Minimum SIP Investment:** ₹500
- **Minimum Lump-sum Investment:** ₹500
- **Stamp Duty:** 0.005% (from July 1st, 2020)
- **Fund Manager(s):** Anupam Tiwari
- **Launch Date:** 01-Jan-2013

## Investment Objective & Description
The scheme seeks to provide a high level of liquidity with returns commensurate with low risk through a portfolio of money market and debt securities with maturity of up to 91 days.
