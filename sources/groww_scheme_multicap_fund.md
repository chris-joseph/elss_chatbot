# Groww Multicap Fund Direct Growth

## Official Scheme Overview & Key Facts
- **Fund House / AMC:** Groww Mutual Fund
- **Category:** Equity / Multi Cap
- **Benchmark:** Nifty 500 Multicap 50:25:25 Total Return Index
- **Expense Ratio (Direct Growth):** 0.98% (Total Expense Ratio as declared by AMC)
- **Exit Load:** Exit load of 1%, if redeemed within 1 year.
- **Statutory Lock-in Period:** Nil (No lock-in)
- **Minimum SIP Investment:** ₹500
- **Minimum Lump-sum Investment:** ₹500
- **Stamp Duty:** 0.005% (from July 1st, 2020)
- **Fund Manager(s):** Anupam Tiwari
- **Launch Date:** 16-Dec-2024

## Investment Objective & Description
The scheme seeks to achieve long-term capital appreciation by predominantly investing in equity and equity-related instruments of large, mid and small-cap companies.
