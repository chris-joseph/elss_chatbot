# Introduction to Mutual Funds - SEBI Official Guide

## Statutory Definition
Under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996, a mutual fund is a fund established in the form of a trust to raise monies through the sale of units to the public or a section of the public under one or more schemes for investing in securities including money market instruments or gold.

## Governance & Protection
- **Trustees:** The mutual fund trust is governed by Trustees who have the primary responsibility of safeguarding the interests of the unit holders and ensuring compliance with SEBI regulations.
- **Asset Management Company (AMC):** The AMC is appointed by the trustees with the approval of SEBI to manage the funds and float schemes.
- **Custodian:** An independent custodian holds the securities in safe custody for the benefit of the unitholders.
- **Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA):** Handles investor servicing, unit issuance, redemption processing, and statement generation (such as CAS).
