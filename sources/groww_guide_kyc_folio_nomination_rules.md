# Mutual Fund KYC, Folio Numbers, and Nomination Rules

Investing in Indian mutual funds requires compliance with regulatory identity verification, account tracking, and nomination guidelines established by SEBI and AMFI.

## What is Mutual Fund KYC?

Know Your Customer (KYC) is a mandatory one-time customer verification process required by SEBI before any individual can invest in mutual funds or Indian securities markets.
- **Documents Required**: Permanent Account Number (PAN), proof of identity (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID), proof of address, and a verified bank account with IFSC code.
- **One-Time Verification**: Once an investor achieves KYC-compliant status through a SEBI-registered KYC Registration Agency (KRA) or CKYC (Central KYC), they can invest across all mutual fund AMCs in India without repeating the process.
- **CKYC (Central KYC)**: Investors receive a 14-digit CKYC identification number stored with CERSAI.

## What is a Folio Number?

A Folio Number is a unique account number assigned by an Asset Management Company (AMC) to an individual investor's holdings within that specific fund house (similar to a bank account number).
- Multiple investments across different schemes of the same AMC can be held under a single folio number.
- The folio maintains records of all unit balances, transaction history, bank mandates, nominee designations, and contact details.
- When an investor invests in another AMC, a new folio number is generated by that respective fund house.

## Mandatory Nomination Rules

Under SEBI regulations, registering a nominee or explicitly opting out of nomination is mandatory for all individual mutual fund folios:
- **Number of Nominees**: An investor can nominate up to **three individuals** per folio.
- **Allocation Percentage**: The investor must specify the exact percentage of the investment corpus allocated to each nominee (e.g., 50%-50%, or 50%-25%-25%), totaling 100%.
- **Minor Nominees**: A minor can be designated as a nominee, provided the name and contact details of a legal or natural guardian are submitted.
- **Benefits of Nomination**: In the event of the investor's demise, nomination facilitates the smooth transfer of units to the nominee without requiring court probate or succession certificates.

## Investing for Minors

- A mutual fund account can be opened in the name of a minor child (below 18 years of age).
- The minor must be the sole holder; joint holdings are not permitted.
- The account is operated by the natural guardian (father/mother) or a court-appointed legal guardian until the minor turns 18.
- Upon attaining age 18, the folio status must be transitioned from minor to major by submitting the adult's PAN, individual KYC, and bank account details.
