# What is a Mutual Fund?

## Definition & Overview
A mutual fund is an investment vehicle formed when an Asset Management Company (AMC) or fund house pools money from multiple individuals and institutional investors who share a common investment objective.

The pooled corpus is invested by a designated professional fund manager across a portfolio of asset classes such as equity shares, corporate debt bonds, government securities, and money market instruments.

## Key Features of Mutual Funds on Groww
- **Professional Management:** Experienced portfolio managers track markets, perform fundamental and quantitative analysis, and rebalance holdings.
- **Diversification:** Investing in a mutual fund spreads risk across various companies, sectors, and asset classes.
- **Convenient Investment Modes:** Investors can invest via Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) starting at ₹100 to ₹500 per month, or via one-time lump-sum orders.
- **High Liquidity:** Open-ended mutual funds allow investors to redeem their units on any business day at prevailing Net Asset Value (NAV) subject to applicable exit loads.
- **Transparency:** Scheme expense ratios (TER), portfolio holdings, benchmarks, and risk-o-meters are published monthly and daily on AMC and platform websites.
