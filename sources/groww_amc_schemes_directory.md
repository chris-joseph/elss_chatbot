# Complete Directory of Schemes Managed by Groww Asset Management Limited (Groww AMC)

## Overview of Groww Asset Management Limited
Groww Asset Management Limited (formerly Indiabulls Asset Management Company Limited) is the SEBI-registered Asset Management Company (AMC) that manages and operates all mutual fund schemes under Groww Mutual Fund. The fund house offers open-ended mutual fund schemes across active equity, passive index funds, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), fund of funds (FoFs), and debt/liquid categories.

---

## 1. Equity Schemes (Active Management)
Groww AMC manages actively managed equity funds designed for long-term capital appreciation across various market capitalizations and sectors:

1. **Groww ELSS Tax Saver Fund**:
   - **Category:** Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) / Tax Saver
   - **Investment Objective:** Long-term capital growth with tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.
   - **Statutory Lock-in:** 3 Years (mandatory minimum lock-in period).
   - **Benchmark:** NIFTY 500 Total Return Index (TRI).
   - **Expense Ratio (Direct Growth):** 1.47%.
   - **Exit Load:** Nil.
   - **Minimum SIP Investment:** ₹500.

2. **Groww Multicap Fund**:
   - **Category:** Equity / Multi Cap
   - **Investment Objective:** Disciplined diversification across market caps, maintaining a statutory minimum of 25% each in large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap companies.
   - **Benchmark:** NIFTY 500 Multicap 50:25:25 Total Return Index (TRI).
   - **Expense Ratio (Direct Growth):** 0.98%.
   - **Exit Load:** 1% if redeemed within 365 days; Nil thereafter.
   - **Minimum SIP Investment:** ₹500.

3. **Groww Banking & Financial Services Fund**:
   - **Category:** Equity / Sectoral
   - **Investment Objective:** Focused investment in equities of Indian banking institutions, NBFCs, housing finance companies, insurance providers, and capital market entities.
   - **Benchmark:** NIFTY Financial Services Total Return Index (TRI).
   - **Expense Ratio (Direct Growth):** 1.42%.
   - **Exit Load:** 1% if redeemed within 30 days; Nil thereafter.
   - **Minimum SIP Investment:** ₹500.

4. **Groww Large Cap Fund**:
   - **Category:** Equity / Large Cap
   - **Investment Objective:** Capital growth by investing predominantly in top 100 Indian companies by market capitalization.
   - **Benchmark:** NIFTY 100 Total Return Index (TRI).

5. **Groww Small Cap Fund**:
   - **Category:** Equity / Small Cap
   - **Investment Objective:** Long-term capital appreciation through high-growth potential small-cap companies (251st company onwards by market capitalization).
   - **Benchmark:** NIFTY Smallcap 250 Total Return Index (TRI).

6. **Groww Value Fund**:
   - **Category:** Equity / Value Fund
   - **Investment Objective:** Value-oriented investment strategy identifying fundamentally strong undervalued businesses.
   - **Benchmark:** NIFTY 500 Total Return Index (TRI).

## 2. Passive Index Funds, ETFs & Fund of Funds (FoF)
Groww AMC is known for low-cost passive index funds and thematic FoFs tracking broad-market and sectoral benchmarks:

1. **Groww Nifty Total Market Index Fund**:
   - **Category:** Index Fund / Large, Mid, Small & Micro Cap
   - **Investment Objective:** Passive replication of the Nifty Total Market Index comprising 750 Indian stocks across all market capitalizations.
   - **Benchmark:** Nifty Total Market Total Return Index (TRI).
   - **Expense Ratio (Direct Growth):** 0.66%.
   - **Exit Load:** 0.25% if redeemed within 7 days; Nil thereafter.
   - **Minimum SIP Investment:** ₹500.

2. **Groww Nifty 50 Index Fund**:
   - **Category:** Index Fund / Large Cap
   - **Investment Objective:** Tracking India's premier 50 large-cap blue-chip stocks.
   - **Benchmark:** Nifty 50 Total Return Index (TRI).

3. **Groww Nifty Smallcap 250 Index Fund**:
   - **Category:** Index Fund / Small Cap
   - **Investment Objective:** Low-cost passive exposure to 250 small-cap equities.
   - **Benchmark:** Nifty Smallcap 250 Total Return Index (TRI).

4. **Groww Nifty Non-Cyclical Consumer Index Fund**:
   - **Category:** Thematic Index Fund / FMCG & Consumer Durables
   - **Benchmark:** Nifty Non-Cyclical Consumer Index.

5. **Groww Commodity & Thematic ETF FoFs**:
   - **Groww Gold ETF FoF**: Fund of funds investing in units of Gold ETFs.
   - **Groww Silver ETF FoF**: Fund of funds investing in units of Silver ETFs.
   - **Groww Nifty EV & New Age Automotive ETF FoF**: Thematic vehicle tracking electric vehicle and mobility innovators.
   - **Groww Nifty India Defence ETF FoF**: Thematic vehicle tracking Indian defence manufacturing and aerospace companies.

## 3. Debt & Liquid Schemes
Groww AMC provides debt and fixed-income mutual fund schemes tailored for capital preservation, liquidity management, and short-term surplus deployment:

1. **Groww Liquid Fund**:
   - **Category:** Debt / Liquid Fund
   - **Investment Objective:** High liquidity with minimal interest rate risk by investing in debt and money market securities maturing within 91 days.
   - **Benchmark:** CRISIL Liquid Debt A-I Index.
   - **Expense Ratio (Direct Growth):** 0.11%.
   - **Exit Load:** SEBI tiered exit load schedule from 0.0070% down to Nil after day 7.
   - **Statutory Lock-in:** Nil (No lock-in).
   - **Minimum SIP Investment:** ₹500.

2. **Groww Overnight Fund**:
   - **Category:** Debt / Overnight Fund
   - **Investment Objective:** Daily liquidity with overnight maturity securities (TREPS / Reverse Repo).
   - **Exit Load:** Nil.

3. **Groww Short Duration Fund**:
   - **Category:** Debt / Short Duration
   - **Investment Objective:** Optimal returns across 1 to 3 year debt instruments.
