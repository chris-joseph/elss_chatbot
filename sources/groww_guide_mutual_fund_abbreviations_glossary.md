# Complete Glossary of Mutual Fund Abbreviations and Acronyms

This glossary provides official definitions, full forms, and regulatory meanings for common abbreviations and acronyms used in the Indian mutual fund industry.

## Core Fund Concepts and Regulatory Acronyms

- **MF (Mutual Fund)**: A professionally managed investment vehicle that pools money from multiple investors to purchase a diversified portfolio of stocks, bonds, or other securities.
- **AMC (Asset Management Company)**: A SEBI-registered company that manages the mutual fund's investment portfolio, launches schemes, and appoints professional fund managers.
- **AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India)**: The industry trade association of all SEBI-registered asset management companies in India, promoting investor education and ethical standards.
- **SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India)**: The apex statutory regulatory body governing securities markets and mutual funds in India to protect investor interests.
- **NAV (Net Asset Value)**: The per-unit market value of a mutual fund scheme, calculated daily by dividing the net assets of the scheme by the total number of outstanding units.
- **AUM (Assets Under Management)**: The total cumulative market value of all investments and cash held by a mutual fund scheme or asset management company.
- **TER (Total Expense Ratio)**: The annual operating fee percentage charged by the AMC to manage the mutual fund, deducted daily from the scheme's Net Asset Value.
- **NFO (New Fund Offer)**: The initial launch period during which an Asset Management Company offers units of a newly created mutual fund scheme to the public, typically at ₹10 per unit.
- **FOF (Fund of Funds)**: A mutual fund scheme that invests its capital into other mutual fund schemes rather than directly investing in individual stocks or bonds.
- **ETF (Exchange Traded Fund)**: A marketable security that tracks an underlying index, commodity, or basket of assets and trades intraday on stock exchanges like individual shares.
- **ISIN (International Securities Identification Number)**: A globally unique 12-character alphanumeric code used to identify specific mutual fund units and securities.

## Investment Modes and Transaction Terms

- **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)**: An investment method allowing an investor to invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals (monthly or weekly) into a mutual fund scheme.
- **STP (Systematic Transfer Plan)**: A facility enabling an investor to periodically transfer a predetermined amount from one mutual fund scheme (usually liquid/debt) to another scheme (usually equity) within the same AMC.
- **SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)**: A facility allowing an investor to redeem a predetermined fixed amount periodically from an existing mutual fund investment into their bank account.
- **IDCW (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal)**: The official term for the dividend option, where an AMC distributes periodic capital payouts to unit holders out of realized scheme profits.
- **eNACH (Electronic National Automated Clearing House)**: An electronic auto-debit mandate platform operated by NPCI allowing bank accounts to be debited automatically for recurring SIP installments.
- **OTM (One Time Mandate)**: A one-time registration with a bank authorizing automated debits for mutual fund lump sums and SIPs up to a specified daily limit.
- **UPI (Unified Payments Interface)**: An instant real-time payment system developed by NPCI used for funding mutual fund purchases and automated recurring mandates.

## Scheme Types and Asset Class Abbreviations

- **ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)**: A diversified equity mutual fund offering tax deductions up to ₹1.5 Lakh under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, subject to a statutory 3-year lock-in period.
- **TRI (Total Return Index)**: A benchmark index that reflects both the capital price appreciation of underlying shares and the dividends paid by them, mandated by SEBI for all mutual fund comparisons.
- **PRI (Price Return Index)**: An index reflecting only the capital price movements of constituent shares, excluding cash dividends.
- **Gilt (Government Securities Fund)**: A debt mutual fund that invests exclusively in sovereign government securities issued by the Reserve Bank of India, carrying sovereign safety with zero credit risk.
- **FD (Fixed Deposit)**: A fixed-tenure bank deposit that pays a guaranteed rate of interest, distinct from market-linked mutual funds.
- **PPF (Public Provident Fund)**: A 15-year statutory government-backed small savings scheme providing guaranteed tax-exempt interest under Section 80C.
- **NPS (National Pension System)**: A government-sponsored voluntary retirement savings program combining equity, corporate debt, and government bond asset classes.

## Legal, Disclosure, and Account Documents

- **SID (Scheme Information Document)**: The statutory legal charter of a mutual fund scheme containing its investment objectives, asset allocation bands, investment strategies, fees, and fund manager details.
- **KIM (Key Information Memorandum)**: A concise, investor-friendly summary of the Scheme Information Document containing critical operational facts and risks, attached to application forms.
- **SAI (Statement of Additional Information)**: The statutory document detailing corporate, operational, and legal disclosures about the mutual fund trust, sponsor, and trustees.
- **CAS (Consolidated Account Statement)**: A single consolidated monthly document detailing all mutual fund holdings and depository transactions across all AMCs linked to an investor's PAN.
- **eCAS (Electronic Consolidated Account Statement)**: A digital, password-protected electronic version of the Consolidated Account Statement downloaded directly from CAMS, KFintech, or depositories.
- **SoA (Statement of Account)**: The non-demat, physical mode of holding mutual fund units directly with the AMC/RTA under a dedicated folio number.
- **KYC (Know Your Customer)**: The mandatory customer identification and verification process required by SEBI before investing in Indian financial markets.
- **CKYC (Central Know Your Customer)**: A centralized registry managed by CERSAI storing an investor's verified KYC records, eliminating repetitive verification across financial intermediaries.
- **KRA (KYC Registration Agency)**: A SEBI-registered agency (such as CAMS, CVL, or NDML) that maintains and centralizes KYC records of investors.
- **PAN (Permanent Account Number)**: A 10-digit alphanumeric identifier issued by the Indian Income Tax Department, mandatory for all mutual fund investments.
- **FATCA (Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act)**: A statutory tax compliance declaration required to ensure foreign citizens and tax residents comply with international tax reporting standards.

## Operational Entities and Depositories

- **RTA (Registrar and Transfer Agent)**: An institution appointed by the AMC (such as CAMS or KFintech) to handle investor record-keeping, unit allotments, redemptions, and statement generation.
- **CAMS (Computer Age Management Services)**: India's largest mutual fund registrar and transfer agent servicing majority mutual fund houses.
- **KFintech (KFin Technologies Limited)**: A leading SEBI-registered registrar and transfer agent providing investor servicing to several major Indian AMCs.
- **NSDL (National Securities Depository Limited)**: A premier Indian depository holding securities and mutual fund units electronically in demat form.
- **CDSL (Central Depository Services Limited)**: A major SEBI-registered Indian securities depository holding demat accounts.
- **DP (Depository Participant)**: An agent of the depository (like Groww, Zerodha, or a bank) through whom investors open and maintain demat accounts.
- **BOID (Beneficiary Owner Identification)**: A 16-digit unique number assigned to an investor's demat account with CDSL or NSDL.

## Taxation and Statutory Terms

- **STCG (Short-Term Capital Gains)**: Profit realized from selling mutual fund units held for less than the statutory long-term holding period (12 months for equity funds).
- **LTCG (Long-Term Capital Gains)**: Profit realized from selling mutual fund units held longer than the statutory long-term holding period (over 12 months for equity funds).
- **STT (Securities Transaction Tax)**: A statutory direct tax of 0.001% levied by the Government of India exclusively on the redemption of equity mutual fund units.
- **TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)**: Statutory tax deducted before distributing payments, applicable to IDCW dividend income when annual payouts exceed ₹5,000.
- **ITR (Income Tax Return)**: The official annual tax filing form submitted to the Income Tax Department reporting income and mutual fund capital gains.

## Performance and Risk Metrics

- **CAGR (Compounded Annual Growth Rate)**: The annualized constant rate of return that represents the geometric progression of an investment over periods exceeding one year.
- **XIRR (Extended Internal Rate of Return)**: The standardized financial metric used to calculate annualized returns on investments involving multiple periodic cash flows at irregular or regular intervals, such as SIPs and STPs.
- **YTM (Yield to Maturity)**: The total anticipated annual rate of return earned on a debt mutual fund portfolio assuming all bonds are held until maturity.
- **PE (Price-to-Earnings Ratio)**: A valuation ratio comparing the market price of portfolio stocks to their earnings per share.
- **PB (Price-to-Book Ratio)**: A valuation ratio comparing the market value of portfolio companies to their book value of equity.
- **SD (Standard Deviation)**: A statistical dispersion metric measuring the historical volatility of a mutual fund's periodic returns.

## Investor Grievance and Dispute Redressal

- **SCORES (SEBI Complaints Redress System)**: An online centralized grievance redressal portal operated by SEBI enabling investors to lodge and track complaints against AMCs and intermediaries.
- **ODR (Online Dispute Resolution)**: A digital mediation and arbitration platform established under SEBI guidelines (SMART ODR) to resolve financial market disputes between investors and market participants.
