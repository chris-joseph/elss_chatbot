# Growth Option vs IDCW (Dividend) Option

When investing in any mutual fund scheme, an investor must select one of two plan options: **Growth Option** or **IDCW (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal) Option**. Both options invest in the exact same scheme portfolio, but differ in how profits and earnings are handled.

## Growth Option

Under the Growth Option, all profits, interest income, and capital appreciation realized by the mutual fund are retained within the fund and reinvested into the scheme portfolio.
- **Compounding Benefit**: Earnings generate further earnings, allowing the Net Asset Value (NAV) of the scheme to grow steadily over time.
- **No Cash Payouts**: Investors receive no intermediate dividend payments. Returns are realized only when units are redeemed.
- **Taxation**: Taxes are deferred until the investor actually sells or switches units. Gains are subject to Capital Gains Tax (STCG or LTCG).
- **Suitability**: Ideal for long-term wealth creation and investors who do not require regular income.

## IDCW Option (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal)

Formerly known as the "Dividend Option", SEBI renamed it to **IDCW** to clarify that payouts consist of both accumulated scheme income and capital distribution from the investor's unit capital.
- **Periodic Payouts**: When the fund house declares an IDCW payout, cash is transferred directly to the investor's bank account.
- **NAV Drops After Payout**: When an IDCW payout is distributed, the fund's NAV drops by the exact amount paid out per unit (plus applicable taxes).
- **No Guaranteed Payouts**: An AMC is not legally required to declare IDCW distributions regularly; payouts depend strictly on distributable surplus.
- **Two Sub-Options**:
  1. *IDCW Payout*: Cash is credited directly to the registered bank account.
  2. *IDCW Reinvestment*: Payout amount is automatically used to purchase additional units of the scheme at the ex-dividend NAV.

## Taxation Differences Between Growth and IDCW

- **Growth Option Taxation**: Gains are taxed only upon redemption as capital gains (Equity funds: 20% STCG if held $\le 12$ months; 12.5% LTCG above ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year if held $> 12$ months).
- **IDCW Option Taxation**: Any IDCW payout received is added directly to the investor's taxable annual income and taxed at their individual income tax slab rate. Furthermore, the AMC deducts 10% TDS if the total annual IDCW payout exceeds ₹5,000 for a resident investor.
