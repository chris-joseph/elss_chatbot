# Transacting in Mutual Funds: Timings, Cut-Offs, and Settlement

## When Can You Buy and Sell Mutual Funds?

Investors can place purchase and redemption orders on the Groww platform **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**. However, actual order processing, fund transfer, and unit allotment occur only on official **business days** (Monday through Friday, excluding exchange and banking holidays).

Orders placed on weekends or market holidays are processed on the next official business day.

## SEBI Cut-Off Timings and NAV Allocation

The Net Asset Value (NAV) applicable to an order depends on SEBI-mandated cut-off timings and the realization of funds in the AMC's bank account.

### 1. Equity and Debt Mutual Funds
- **Cut-Off Time**: **2:00 PM** (IST) on business days.
- **Fund Realization Rule**: Under SEBI guidelines, the same day's closing NAV is allotted **only if** both the order is submitted and the investment money is credited to the AMC bank account before 2:00 PM.
- If money is credited after 2:00 PM, or on a non-business day, units are allotted at the next business day's closing NAV.

### 2. Liquid and Overnight Funds
- **Cut-Off Time**: **12:00 PM** (Noon) on business days.
- If the application and funds are received before 12:00 PM, the previous day's closing NAV is allotted.
- If received after 12:00 PM, the same day's closing NAV is allotted.

### 3. Redemption (Selling Units) Cut-Off
- **Cut-Off Time**: **2:00 PM** for equity and debt schemes; **12:00 PM** for liquid schemes.
- Orders placed before cut-off receive that business day's closing NAV.
- Orders placed after cut-off receive the next business day's closing NAV.

## Settlement Cycles: When Do You Get Your Money or Units?

- **Purchase Allotment**: Mutual fund units are allotted within **T+1 to T+2 business days** after payment realization. Once allotted, units appear in the investor's Groww portfolio and CAS.
- **Equity Scheme Redemption**: Redemption proceeds are credited directly to the investor's verified primary bank account within **T+2 business days**.
- **Liquid and Overnight Scheme Redemption**: Payouts are credited within **T+1 business day**.
- **Lock-in Restrictions**: Units in ELSS schemes cannot be redeemed until the completion of their mandatory 3-year lock-in period from the allotment date.
