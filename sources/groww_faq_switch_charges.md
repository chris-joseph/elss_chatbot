# What are the charges to switch from regular to direct

Groww does not charge users for switching from regular to direct funds. However, if you place your switch request during the exit load period of a fund, you will be charged both exit load and tax by the fund house if applicable for your fund. If switching after the exit load period, no charges are applicable.
