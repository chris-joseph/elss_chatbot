# Asset Management Company (AMC)

## What is an AMC?

An Asset Management Company (AMC) is a SEBI-registered corporate entity responsible for managing the pooled investments of a mutual fund. The AMC designs, launches, and operates mutual fund schemes in accordance with SEBI regulations and the investment objectives outlined in each Scheme Information Document (SID).

For example, Groww Asset Management Limited is the AMC that operates and manages Groww Mutual Fund schemes.

### Schemes Managed by Groww AMC
Groww Asset Management Limited offers a diverse portfolio of mutual fund schemes:
- **Active Equity Schemes:** Groww ELSS Tax Saver Fund, Groww Multicap Fund, Groww Banking & Financial Services Fund, Groww Large Cap Fund, Groww Small Cap Fund, and Groww Value Fund.
- **Index Funds & FoFs:** Groww Nifty Total Market Index Fund, Groww Nifty 50 Index Fund, Groww Nifty Smallcap 250 Index Fund, Groww Gold/Silver ETF FoFs.
- **Debt & Liquid Schemes:** Groww Liquid Fund, Groww Overnight Fund, and Groww Short Duration Fund.

## Who Runs Mutual Funds: The 3-Tier Governance Structure

Indian mutual funds operate under a strict three-tier fiduciary structure mandated by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) to ensure investor money is protected:

1. **The Sponsor**: The promoter entity that establishes the mutual fund trust (similar to the promoter of a company). The sponsor must satisfy stringent SEBI net-worth and integrity criteria.
2. **The Board of Trustees / Trustee Company**: The independent fiduciary entity that holds the assets of the mutual fund schemes in trust for the benefit of unit holders. Trustees oversee AMC operations, ensure compliance with SEBI guidelines, and ensure unit holders' interests are safeguarded.
3. **The Asset Management Company (AMC)**: The operational investment manager appointed by the Trustees. The AMC hires professional fund managers, research analysts, and compliance teams to make portfolio buying and selling decisions.

Additional independent institutions support this structure:
- **Custodian**: An independent SEBI-registered bank or financial institution (e.g., Deutsche Bank, ICICI Bank) that physically and electronically holds the fund's securities in safe custody, completely separate from the AMC's balance sheet.
- **Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA)**: Operational agencies like CAMS and KFintech that handle unit allotment, KYC records, statements, and redemptions.

## Role of Professional Fund Managers

Within an AMC, dedicated fund managers and research analysts actively monitor macro trends, conduct company research, and execute portfolio trades:
- Fund managers execute trades strictly within the asset allocation mandates defined in the scheme's SID.
- They ensure diversification across companies, sectors, and asset classes to control unsystematic risk.
- They are subject to rigorous SEBI conflict-of-interest regulations, personal trading restrictions, and insider trading prohibitions.

## Regulatory Oversight by SEBI

Every AMC in India is regulated and inspected by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI):
- AMCs must maintain a minimum statutory net worth (at least ₹50 Crore) to operate.
- SEBI caps the maximum Total Expense Ratio (TER) an AMC can charge investors.
- AMCs must publish complete scheme portfolios monthly, disclose riskometers, and compute Net Asset Values (NAVs) every business day.
